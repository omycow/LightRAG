Key Strategic Insights

⚡

Model Scaling Paradigm Shift

Pre-training scaling has plateaued. Innovation has shifted to post-training techniques (RLHF, DPO) and inference-time compute. DeepSeek R1 proved that smaller, efficient models can rival trillion-parameter architectures.

Efficiency > Scale
🤖

Agentic AI: The Next Platform

40% of enterprise apps will embed AI agents by end of 2026 (Gartner). Average ROI of 171%, but only 2% deployed at scale. 40%+ of agentic projects may be cancelled by 2027.

171% ROI
💻

AI Coding: Fastest Growth Vector

Claude Code grew from 3% to 18% share in 9 months. Anthropic's ARR surged from $1B to $47B in 15 months, driven primarily by coding use cases. 84% of developers now use AI tools.

6x Growth in 9mo
